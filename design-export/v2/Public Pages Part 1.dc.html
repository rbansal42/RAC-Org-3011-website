DISEASE PREVENTION AND TREATMENT

Mahadan blood donation camp

Nine clubs ran a single-day camp at four venues across South Delhi with Rotary Blood Bank. Donors were pre-registered through club WhatsApp groups to keep queues short.

The camp ran from eight in the morning until six, with registration desks at each venue staffed by two Rotaractors on rotation. Rotary Blood Bank provided the collection teams and transport; clubs handled outreach, refreshments and the donor queue.

Pre-registration through WhatsApp turned out to be the difference. In previous years donors arrived unannounced and waited upwards of an hour; this time the longest recorded wait was eleven minutes. Clubs planning a similar camp should budget one registration volunteer per forty pre-registered donors.

Follow-up: donors who deferred on medical grounds were added to a list for the next camp rather than being turned away without a next step.
