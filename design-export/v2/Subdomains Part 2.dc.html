WHY CAPACITY, NOT A YES/NO

An opt-in flag tells the district nothing when a delegation of eight lands and someone has to place them by Friday.

Four delegates, homestay, January to March is an answer. "Interested" is a conversation that has to happen again from scratch every time.

Not the sister-club form

Easy to conflate, since both sit under International Services. Sister-club agreements are a twinning contract between two clubs and live on the main site at /resources/sister-club. RIDE is a district-run exchange. Two different things, deliberately kept on different domains.
