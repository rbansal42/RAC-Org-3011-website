A CHANGE FROM TODAY'S BUILD

The portal currently refuses DAC accounts outright — LoginModal.jsx returns "DAC Members do not have access to District or Club Portals" for dac_member, while PortalPage.jsx already lets the secretariat address announcements to them.

So a DAC broadcast today has no inbox to land in. This screen is that inbox, plus the read-only numbers a committee needs to advise on. Nothing here writes.

WHAT A DAC MEMBER CANNOT SEE

Any individual club's points

Advice should not turn into scoring

Officer phone numbers and blood groups

Not needed to advise

The scoring queue

Officers score; the committee reviews the method

Papers, not dashboards

Most DAC members are past DRRs who sit a handful of times a year. The thing they actually need is the document pack before a sitting, which is why an announcement carrying attachments is the loudest element on the page and the statistics are a quiet band above it.

THE SAME SCREEN, THREE ROLES

President

Everything here, plus Start the report, the month’s score, and the query inbox

Secretary

Everything the president sees, and can file on their behalf

Member — shown here

This view. Read the month, add a photo, see what’s coming

One layout, three capability sets. Officers get actions added to this page rather than a different page, so a member who becomes president next July already knows where everything is.

A MEMBER NEVER SEES

The club’s points

Scores are for office bearers to act on, not the group chat to argue about

Other clubs’ anything

Same rule as everywhere else in the portal

The president database

Contact details stay with officers

Why give members a login at all

Because the missing photo problem is a member problem. The president wasn't holding the camera. Handing three hundred members one small, obvious task — attach the picture you already have — fixes more showcase entries than any reminder to office bearers, and it costs one read-only screen.
