Within about a week. They may tighten the wording; they will not change what you claim

4

It appears on the showcase

With your club named, and your name if you want it

Why a member submits, not the president

The avenue directors run the projects. Routing every showcase entry through the president makes one person the bottleneck for thirty-eight people's work, and it means the write-up comes from somebody who was not there.

Publishing still needs an officer, so the gate the district asked for is intact. What changes is who fills the form — and the description is better for it.

The consent tick is not boilerplate

It is the one thing a member can confirm and a district officer cannot. An officer approving a photograph of a school handover has no way to know whether those parents agreed; the person who was in the room does.

Two fields your club owns, not you

Club and joining date are greyed because they decide your scope and your service years. A member editing either would silently move which club's points their attendance counts toward — so those changes go through an officer.

Why filling this in is worth 60 points

The points document awards a club 60 when half its members have entered skills and interests. Saying so plainly is more honest than a progress ring nagging you — and it means a president chasing the last seventeen members has a reason to give them.

MAIL AND PUSH ARE SEPARATE COLUMNS

Because they cost differently. Email runs on pooled free tiers of about seven hundred a day across three providers; push is effectively free but only reaches people who allowed it.

A single on/off per category would force the district to send both or neither, and 312 members × six categories is how a free tier gets exhausted by a Tuesday.

THE COMPULSORY EXCEPTION

Stated on the screen rather than buried, because a portal that silently overrides a preference loses the argument the first time somebody notices. Compulsory means an announcement the secretariat marked as requiring a reply — roughly one a month.

Deletion that tells the truth

Most products say "your data will be deleted" and keep aggregates anyway. Naming the two records that survive, and saying they carry no name, is both accurate and less alarming than the vague version.
