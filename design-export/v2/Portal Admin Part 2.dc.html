MEMBERS CAN LOG THEIR OWN

A member submits hours and a description; their president or secretary approves it before it reaches this table.

That approval step is the whole design. Without it this becomes a self-service points tap, and the DRR ends up auditing a hundred claims a month instead of recognising four people who genuinely helped.

No standing public list

Recognition is better when it is chosen. When the DRR wants to spotlight someone it goes on the content side as a highlight — a permanent leaderboard of hours would turn quiet help into competition, which is the same reason club points are private.

A channel, not a ticketing system
