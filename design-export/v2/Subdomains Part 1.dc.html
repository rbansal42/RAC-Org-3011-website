SUBDOMAINS · PART 1 OF 2

Mission 3011, Project Drishti, RCL, Career Bridge, RIDE

Five subdomains, one deployment, one session cookie scoped to the parent domain — so these are the same site wearing a different accent, not five products. Same header shape, same Montserrat, same cream, same card and form components as the main site.

Each project gets exactly one accent colour and nothing else changes: Mission 3011 keeps district maroon, Drishti takes the blue already used for it on /initiatives, RCL a teal, Career Bridge a violet, RIDE a burnt orange. That is enough to tell two dashboards apart at a glance without inventing four visual identities to maintain.

WHILE THE TOTAL LOADS

The gauge holds its ring at zero in the track colour. No spinner, no layout shift, and the number counts up only once — a counter that re-runs on every scroll is the kind of thing that made the old homepage feel heavy.

Why 3,011 is the right target to show

It is the district number, so nobody has to explain it, and it is reachable but not certain — which is the only condition under which a progress bar means anything.

If the district passes it in April, the bar should not cap at 100%. Design for overflow now: the ring fills and the figure keeps climbing, because 3,400 units is a better story than a full circle.
