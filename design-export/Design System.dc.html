DESIGN SYSTEM · LIGHT AND DARK

Components for District 3011

The component set a district site of this size needs — the shadcn inventory, rebuilt in this district's language rather than in a neutral one. Every component appears twice: light on the left, dark on the right, from the same tokens.

Dark mode is not an inversion. The maroon #D81B60 goes muddy on a dark surface, so on dark it lifts to #F0407F and filled buttons take dark ink instead of white. Cream backgrounds become near-black neutrals with a warm cast, never pure grey — a cream-and-maroon site should still feel warm at night.

Metadata

The two rules that make dark mode work here. First, a filled primary button takes dark ink on dark mode — white on #F0407F fails contrast, dark ink on it passes comfortably. Second, elevation stops being a shadow and becomes a surface step: on light, a card is white with a pink-tinted shadow; on dark, a card is #1B1B20 on #131316 and the shadow is only there to separate overlays from the page.

Shadows are pink-tinted rather than grey, which is the single cheapest thing that makes a generic card look like it belongs to this district.

Controls are 44px tall — at every width, not just on phones

A chip, a button and a form field are all a minimum of 44px, set with min-height rather than padding, so height is driven by the target and not by the font. The reason for holding it on desktop too is not touch — it is that two-thirds of this product is a data-entry portal, and a 32px control on a 1440 dashboard reads as denser than the same control on the phone the same officer used that morning. One number is easier to hold to than a breakpoint rule, and the spec calls for mobile-first rather than mobile-only.

Button

button

Every button is 44px tall via min-height, in three widths of padding. One primary per screen, and it is the thing the screen exists for. Outline is the escape hatch beside it; ghost is for actions that undo. Destructive is a deeper maroon rather than a generic red, so a termination still reads as this district and not as a browser alert.
