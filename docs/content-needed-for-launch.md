## The three things that block launch entirely

**1. Privacy policy.** The page currently says, literally, "Placeholder privacy policy. Replace before launch." Since the site collects member names, emails, phone numbers, and photos, this needs real text. If the district has ever had one drafted, send that. If not, this probably needs someone who has done it before, or at least a look from someone comfortable with it.

**2. Terms of service.** Same situation, same placeholder text.

**3. Photos of people.** This is the single biggest visible gap on the site. Every single person on the site currently shows as a coloured circle with their initials. Specifically:
   - **44 past DRRs**, going back to 1984-85, zero photos on file. Even low-resolution scans are fine, the site is built to handle small old photos without stretching them.
   - **14 current district team members** (District Governor, DRR, zone reps and secretaries), zero photos.
   If the full 44 is unrealistic, prioritise the current team and the last 10 years of DRRs.
